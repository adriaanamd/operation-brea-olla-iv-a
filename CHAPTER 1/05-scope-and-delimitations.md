# Scope and Delimitations

This section sets the boundaries of the report so that the evidence is not extended beyond what the activity and its records can support.

## Scope

The report covers the organized webinar titled "CodeCrafters: Tech Beyond the Screen," held on October 12, 2026 according to the manuscript specification and the timestamped evaluation file. The paper studies the activity under the title "Operation Brea Olla IV-A: Planning, Implementation, and Participant Evaluation of an Organized Webinar on AI-Assisted Test-Driven Development for Beginners."

The report focuses on:

- the stated or documented purpose of the webinar;
- the planning and delivery evidence that can be located in the repository;
- the introduction of AI-assisted software development and test-driven development for beginners;
- the technical and instructional artifacts actually supplied;
- the participant-evaluation instrument and its available responses; and
- the relationship between objectives, implementation evidence, participant feedback, and unresolved details.

The current repository contains an evaluation CSV with consent fields, rating items, open-ended questions, and links submitted as attendance evidence. The raw file is treated as read-only. Participant names, email addresses, institution fields, and attendance links will not be reproduced in the manuscript. Any summaries will be de-identified and generated through the Chapter 3 analysis workflow before they are used in Chapter 4.

## Delimitations

The report is delimited to one webinar and does not claim to represent all webinars, all beginner programmers, all SDET roles, or all AI coding assistants. It does not evaluate the accuracy, security, or performance of an AI model as a standalone technical benchmark. It also does not constitute a psychometric validation of the evaluation instrument.

The available records do not yet verify the following details:

- the hosting platform, venue, and access arrangement;
- the organizers, facilitator, presenter, and assigned responsibilities;
- the approved program and exact opening and closing times;
- the final slide deck, presenter script, repository, or demonstration code;
- whether Vitest, a particular AI coding agent, or a particular continuous-integration service was used during the session;
- the attendance denominator and the relationship between attendance evidence and evaluation responses; and
- any pre-test, post-test, performance task, or delayed follow-up measure of learning.

These items remain **[TO VERIFY: obtain the official program, approval record, final materials, technical artifacts, and attendance basis]**. Until they are supplied or independently checked, the report will not state them as facts.

## Limits of interpretation

Participant ratings and written comments will be interpreted as self-reported experience. They may describe perceived clarity, relevance, usefulness, or satisfaction, but they do not establish objective learning, improved code quality, secure software, increased productivity, or long-term adoption of TDD. Because the design is a bounded post-activity evaluation, causal claims and broad generalizations are outside the scope of the report.
