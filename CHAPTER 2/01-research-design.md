# Research design

This report used a convergent mixed-methods, nonexperimental, post-activity evaluation design. The quantitative and qualitative strands came from the same post-webinar evaluation export and were analyzed separately before being brought together in a mixed-methods evidence matrix. The design was selected because the evaluation asked both how respondents rated the session and what they described in their own words.

The quantitative strand described 13 experience statements on a five-point Likert scale. Overall rating and satisfaction were retained as separate single-item indicators rather than combined with the primary item set. The qualitative strand used analyst-coded thematic analysis of four open-ended prompts. The two strands were integrated at interpretation: patterns in the rating summaries were compared with the content of the coded comments, while areas of disagreement were retained as limitations rather than forced into a single conclusion.

Because the evaluation was collected after one activity and did not include a control group, pretest, posttest, or objective performance measure, it was not designed to estimate causal learning effects. The results describe reported experience and feedback from the available respondents.
