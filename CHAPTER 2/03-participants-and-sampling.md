# Participants and sampling

The dataset contained 52 available evaluation rows. These rows were treated as available responses, not as a verified census of webinar attendees. The attendance denominator, invitation denominator, and response rate were not present in the supplied export; consequently, the sampling frame and nonresponse pattern could not be estimated. The analysis used row-order identifiers (`R001`–`R052`) created for reproducibility and de-identification.

The sampling basis was therefore availability-based: every row in the supplied export that passed the structural and rating validation rules was included. No respondent was removed because of institution, timestamp, or open-ended response content. The raw export was retained as read-only input, while derived files omitted institution and timestamp fields.

This sampling decision limits transferability. The results describe the respondents represented in the export and should not be interpreted as estimates for all registrants, all attendees, or a wider student population.
