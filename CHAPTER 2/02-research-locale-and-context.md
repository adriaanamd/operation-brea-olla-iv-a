# Research locale and context

The analyzed artifact was a timestamped evaluation export for the CodeCrafters webinar identified in the file name as “Tech Beyond the Screen – Day 1 Afternoon Session.” The timestamps place the response collection on October 12, 2026 (GMT+8). The export documents an online-event evaluation context, but it does not establish the platform, virtual venue, host organization, presenter roster, session schedule, or technical configuration. Those details remain **[TO VERIFY: confirm from the official program, recording, or organizer documentation]**.

The context was therefore bounded to what the evaluation instrument could support: respondents rated the session experience and supplied short comments about learning value, preferred aspects, possible improvements, and additional suggestions. Institutional names were present in the raw export but were excluded from the de-identified analysis outputs and from the manuscript tables.
