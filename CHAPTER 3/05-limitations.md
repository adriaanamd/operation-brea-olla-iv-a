# Limitations

Several limitations constrain the interpretation of the results. The evidence came from one webinar and 52 available responses, with no verified attendance denominator or response rate. The sampling basis was therefore availability-based, and the results may not represent all registrants or attendees. The evaluation was post-event and self-reported; it did not measure objective competence, delayed retention, transfer, or causal learning effects.

The primary Likert items were normalized for two response-pattern cases using an internally specified rule. Although the adjustment is documented at field level, it is still an analyst decision and should be examined in a sensitivity analysis in future work. The instrument was not psychometrically validated, and the analysis does not establish that the 13 items form a single scale.

The qualitative codes were produced by one analyst. No independent coder or inter-rater agreement estimate was available, and multiple coding means that theme assignments are not mutually exclusive. Short, blank, or non-substantive answers also limited the depth of the thematic evidence.
