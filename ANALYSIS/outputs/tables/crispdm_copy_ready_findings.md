### Key performance highlights
- The analytic sample contained 52 submitted responses. The 13-item Likert matrix was summarized after the pre-specified normalization rule flagged 2 respondent row(s).
- Overall satisfaction: 36/52 respondents (69.23%) selected 5/5; 98.08% selected 4 or 5.
- Overall session rating: 33/52 respondents (63.46%) selected 5/5; 98.08% selected 4 or 5.
- Highest primary dimension: Presentation materials were clear and organized (mean 4.73; agreement 98.08%; top-box 75.00%).
- Lowest primary dimension: Speakers explained the topics clearly (mean 4.56; agreement 96.15%; top-box 59.62%).
- Among respondents selecting the strongest speaker-clarity category, the overall-rating 5/5 rate was 83.87% (26/31); this is a descriptive cross-tab, not evidence of a causal effect.
- The ordinal association between speaker clarity and overall rating was Spearman rho = 0.5459 across 52 complete cases; interpret this as descriptive, not causal.

### Qualitative evidence and action cues
- Theme frequencies are coded assignments and may include multiple codes per respondent answer; they are not counts of objectively learned outcomes.
- The institutional distribution and prompt-level response coverage are available in `crispdm_institution_summary.csv` and `crispdm_data_profile.csv`.

### Interpretation boundary
- These results describe post-event perceptions from this respondent set. They do not establish objective learning, causal impact, psychometric validity, or population-level generalizability.
